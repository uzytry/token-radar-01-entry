How the board was chosen

All 40 protocols on the house watchlist are on the board — the watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling. The other 110 rows are the model's own top 110 by implied value among the 138 pre-token protocols below the $50M floor that pass the watchlist's exclusion rule (no CEX, bridge, liquid staking or restaking, no versioned or wrapped/staked names, no exchange-named products). Protocols above the floor that the house left off the watchlist are not ranked: the house excluded them for a reason its record carries and the snapshot does not.

How farm plans are built

Each protocol gets 8 steps, each with a cost and a time. Capital follows TVL ($50–$250); gas follows the protocol's chain (Ethereum about $6, Bitcoin about $2, Solana about $0.02, other chains about $0.15); the hold follows age (30–90 days); both actions follow the category. The totals strip in each detail view is computed from the same numbers as the steps — including every weekly repetition — so the plan's true cost is visible, not just its first week.

What the snapshot cannot tell us

The snapshot carries no revenue, no fees, no 30-day momentum (only 7 days), no TVL per chain, no team or funding record, and no airdrop flag. A protocol's listing date is missing for 113 of 758 protocols; those take 365 days. A protocol is said to record no token, never to have none — its DefiLlama record may simply not carry it. The implied value is this entry's own claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is; it does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
